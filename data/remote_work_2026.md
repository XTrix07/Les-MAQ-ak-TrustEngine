# Official Circular: Remote Work and Office Expense Allowance (2026)
- Date: 01/10/2026
- Author: Sarah Martin (Lead Payroll Specialist & Legal Expert)
- Country: Belgium
- Status: Validated and Active

Starting January 2026, the net flat-rate allowance for remote work expenses in Belgium is updated to **€154.74 per month** for full-time employees.
Mandatory condition: Remote work must be framed by an amendment to the employment contract or a registered company policy.
*Expert note: Do not take into account the old 2022 salary grids.*
