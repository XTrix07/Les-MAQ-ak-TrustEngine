# Internal Memo: Flat-rate Remote Work Allowance (2022)
- Date: 03/15/2022
- Author: Jean Dupont (Former HR Manager)
- Country: Belgium
- Status: Archived / Obsolete

The net flat-rate allowance for remote workers is set at a maximum of €129.48 per month. It covers office expenses, electricity, and internet connection. No additional supporting documents are required if the employee works remotely on a structured basis (at least 5 days per month).
